Rules
• Each side has 1 King, 4 Blockers, and 10 walls.
• Each turn do one of: move your King one square (incl. diagonals), move one of your Blockers one square, place a wall from your pool, smash an unprotected wall beside your King, or relocate one of your walls to a new edge.
• Smash gives back one wall to the wall's original owner, capped at their max.
• Losing a Blocker reduces your max walls by 2. If you have more walls than the new max, your pool is discarded first, then you choose which on-board walls to remove.
• A wall is protected if any Blocker (either side) touches any part of it, including a single shared corner.
• After every action, any Blocker with no free adjacent square is destroyed.
• A King is only trapped if it has no legal moves and no smashable walls.
• Threefold repetition is a draw.

PvP flip: in 👤 vs 👤 mode, enable "Flip board between turns" so the board rotates so each player sees their own King at the bottom.
